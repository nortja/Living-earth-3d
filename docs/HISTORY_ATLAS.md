# Historical teaching windows

The atlas filters approximate modern globe markers. It does not reconstruct ancient terrain or buildings, show historical borders, date every occupation phase or assert exact founding/abandonment years. Negative display years are BCE; year zero is omitted. A site can continue to be inhabited or used after its selected teaching window.

Checked 2026-10-20. Notes are original concise summaries. Sources support the historical context; interval boundaries are deliberately rounded editorial filters. Inclusive boundaries are tested. Modern photographs, when available, are explicitly labelled as photographs rather than reconstructions. The catalog remains introductory and incomplete.
