# Camera channel — 0.12.2

Eight periodic-image feeds open an in-app viewer: Mammoth Hot Springs, Electric Peak, Mount Washburn, Giant Forest, Grand Canyon Yavapai, Look Rock, Alpine Visitor Center and Kīlauea V1. Four other venues open their official provider pages. The catalog supports local name search and image-only filtering. Selecting a camera or arriving on a camera journey opens its actual viewer/provider page; closing the viewer preserves destination context.

The viewer preserves image aspect ratio, credits its provider and distinguishes retrieval time from any source Last-Modified header. Download success does not establish camera capture freshness. Refresh follows the source cadence: slower providers use fifteen minutes, others one minute. Repeated bytes warn after the greater of five minutes or two source periods; failed refresh retains an explicitly labelled old image with bounded backoff. Changing selection clears the old image. Closing, hiding, minimizing or entering screensaver preview pauses/cancels work. No bundled image substitutes for an unavailable camera.

Camera Wall offers 1/2/4/6/9 layouts, eight saved collections and different-camera fill. Duplicate sources share downloads, at most two requests run concurrently, and wall textures are bounded to a 1920-pixel edge while focus preserves up to 4096. Eight unique image feeds cannot fill nine unique-camera tiles; the extra tile stays empty. Hidden/focused-away walls pause.

These are periodically refreshed photographs, not continuous embedded video. Public sources can fail, become seasonal or stop updating. See [source inventory and verification](CAMERA_SOURCES.md) for official venue/endpoint links, dimensions, provider credits, licensing context and refresh cadence. USGS/NPS credit does not imply endorsement. Third-party streams remain official-page links until playback and authorization are resolved.

All four newly added source JPEGs were retrieved, decoded and rendered in the in-app viewer during this build. Existing user confirmation established working Camera Wall/cameras on their earlier Windows release; the expanded release still needs physical Dell/multi-stream performance observation. Authorized continuous playback remains future work.
