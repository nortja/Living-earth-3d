# Earth texture credits

Bundled offline maps from the three.js r160 texture collection:

- https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg
- https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/examples/textures/planets/earth_lights_2048.png
- https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r160/examples/textures/planets/earth_specular_2048.jpg

The upstream repository's MIT license is retained in THREE-LICENSE.txt. These are 2048-pixel-wide maps, not live imagery or terrain. The daytime map includes baked clouds. A higher-resolution, source-verified satellite dataset is still needed for close-up 4K/8K presentation. There is no implication of NASA, Apple, or Google endorsement.
