# Milky Way panorama

Image credit: ESO/S. Brunier. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Source: https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/ . ESO terms: https://www.eso.org/public/copyright/ .

Adapted from the published 6000×3000 TIFF to 4096×2048 and 2048×1024 JPEGs with Lanczos resampling, quality 95, no chroma subsampling. No AI edits or upscaling. Exact source/derivative hashes are in SOURCES.json. Display brightness/orientation are user adjustable. This is a multi-month photographic all-sky composite released in 2009, not a live sky or exterior photograph of our Galaxy. No ESO endorsement. A visible image credit remains with the galaxy scene, including clean exports.
