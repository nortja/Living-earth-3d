# Earth map

`earth_map.svg` is an equirectangular rendering of Natural Earth's 1:110m land polygons, downloaded from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson on 2026-10-15.

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data at https://www.naturalearthdata.com/. Natural Earth data is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

Colors and polar shading are artistic. This simplified map is not satellite imagery, a navigation map, or a representation of political boundaries.
